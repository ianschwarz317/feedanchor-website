FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about FeedAnchor.

Does this violate LinkedIn's Terms of Service?
No. FeedAnchor only saves the post's public URL and the author's visible name — the same information you'd see by right-clicking a link. It doesn't scrape content, automate any actions, or interact with LinkedIn's API. It's functionally equivalent to bookmarking.
Where is my data stored?
Saved posts are stored locally on your device using your browser's built-in storage. Your sign-in details are secured using Google OAuth — we never see your LinkedIn password. Your data is private and never sold.
How does the 20-second timer work?
When you stop scrolling and a post is at least 10% visible on your screen, a silent 20-second countdown begins. If you scroll away before it ends, the timer resets. Only posts you genuinely pause on get saved. Pro users can adjust this to anywhere from 10 to 60 seconds.
Does it work if I close LinkedIn and come back?
Yes — that's exactly what it's built for. Your saved posts persist across sessions and browser restarts. Even after LinkedIn refreshes and wipes your feed, your saved posts stay right in the extension.
What browsers does it support?
FeedAnchor works on Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, and any Chromium-based browser (Brave, Arc, etc.). Firefox support is not available yet.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription?
Yes, anytime. Go to Settings inside the extension and click "Manage" to access the billing portal. There are no cancellation fees and your Pro features remain active until the end of your billing period.
